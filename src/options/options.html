    Organisations

    If your claude.ai sign-in belongs to more than one organisation, each has its own limits. The main one is what the toolbar icon, the alerts and the history are about. Tick others to see their usage side by side with it in the popup.

    Anthropic Console API spend

    The key is kept in this browser's extension storage, unencrypted, and is sent only to api.anthropic.com to read the cost report. The browser asks your permission for that site when you save it.
